Overview

An exploratory analysis of ASOS revenue data, focused on understanding changes over time and interpreting business patterns through visualization.

The questions

  • How does revenue change over the observed period?
  • Where are periods of growth or decline?
  • What patterns become clearer through visualization?

Approach

I used Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Jupyter Notebook to clean the data, examine revenue trends, and create visualizations to support interpretation.

Skills applied

Data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, revenue trend interpretation, and presenting business data through charts.
