Overview

I independently designed and developed Audit Avengers AI during the AWS Global Government Hackathon in Washington, D.C. The prototype helps taxpayers, Treasury employees, and auditors explore complex tax regulations, audit procedures, and Treasury guidance.

The challenge

Regulatory guidance is spread across lengthy documents. Finding a relevant answer is only part of the problem: users also need to see its source and understand when a response needs human review.

What I built

  • Policy-grounded answers: retrieval-based AI connects questions to relevant documents and returns supporting citations.
  • Visible uncertainty: confidence scoring helps communicate when an answer needs closer attention.
  • Human-review escalation: uncertain responses are flagged for review.
  • Audit logging: interactions are recorded to support transparency and accountability.
  • A usable prototype: a Streamlit interface brings the compliance chat and audit log into one experience.

How it works

A user submits a question, the retrieval layer finds relevant policy material, and Amazon Bedrock generates a grounded response. The application presents the answer alongside citations and confidence information, then records the interaction and flags it for review when needed.

My contribution & outcome

I took the project from concept to a working prototype in three days, owning the design, development, and testing. The experience strengthened my ability to translate a government use case into a practical AI application under a tight deadline.

Prototype scope

Built as a hackathon prototype. The repository documents a fallback mode when the temporary Amazon Bedrock environment is unavailable. Confidence scores communicate system uncertainty; they are not a guarantee of regulatory accuracy.
