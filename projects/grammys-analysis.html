Overview

An analysis of website performance around the Grammy Awards, exploring visitor traffic over time, pages per session, and audience demographics.

The questions

  • How does visitor traffic change around the Grammy Awards?
  • How does engagement vary over time?
  • How are visitors distributed across age groups?

Approach

I used Python, Pandas, Plotly, and Jupyter Notebook to clean data, explore time-series patterns, and visualize traffic and engagement metrics. The analysis compares views of the Grammy website and combined site activity.
